# Minecraft Vanilla Statistics Complete Reference

## No-Parameter Statistics (UNTYPED)

### deaths how many times died
DEATHS - Total death count. how many times died, total deaths, death count.

### player kills PVP kills
PLAYER_KILLS - Total player kills. how many players killed, PVP kills.

### mob kills kill mobs how many mobs killed
MOB_KILLS - Total mob kills. how many mobs killed, total mob kills.

### fish caught fishing
FISH_CAUGHT - Total fish caught. how many fish caught, fishing count.

### animals bred breeding
ANIMALS_BRED - Times animals bred. how many animals bred, breeding count.

### leave game logout
LEAVE_GAME - Times left the game. how many times left the game, logout count.

### jumps jump count
JUMP - Total jump count. how many times jumped, jump count.

### drop items how many times dropped
DROP_COUNT - Total times items dropped. how many times dropped items.

### items dropped how many items dropped
DROP - Total number of items dropped. how many items dropped.

### pickup items how many items picked up
PICKUP - Total number of items picked up. how many items picked up.

### play time online time how long played
PLAY_ONE_MINUTE - Total play time (tick, 20 ticks = 1 second). how long played, play time, online time. Note: this value is in ticks, divide by 20 to get seconds, then divide by 3600 to get hours.

### time since death how long since last death
TIME_SINCE_DEATH - Time since last death (tick). how long since last death, how long without dying.

### time since rest how long since last sleep
TIME_SINCE_REST - Time since last rest/sleep (tick). how long since last sleep, how long since last rested.

### sneak time crouch time
SNEAK_TIME - Total sneak time (tick). how long sneaked, how long crouched.

### damage dealt how much damage dealt
DAMAGE_DEALT - Total damage dealt. how much damage dealt in total.

### damage taken how much damage taken
DAMAGE_TAKEN - Total damage taken. how much damage taken in total.

### damage absorbed golden apple
DAMAGE_ABSORBED - Total damage absorbed (golden apple, etc.). how much damage absorbed.

### shield block damage blocked
DAMAGE_BLOCKED_BY_SHIELD - Total damage blocked by shield. how much damage blocked by shield.

### damage dealt absorbed
DAMAGE_DEALT_ABSORBED - Damage dealt but absorbed by target. how much damage was absorbed by the target.

### damage dealt resisted
DAMAGE_DEALT_RESISTED - Damage dealt but reduced by resistance. how much damage was reduced by resistance.

### damage resisted
DAMAGE_RESISTED - Total damage reduced by resistance. how much damage reduced by resistance.

### cake slices eaten cake
CAKE_SLICES_EATEN - Number of cake slices eaten. how much cake eaten.

## Item-Parameter Statistics (ITEM Type)

### craft item crafted how many crafted
CRAFT_ITEM - Requires material parameter (item material name, e.g. DIAMOND_SWORD). Times the specified item was crafted. how many XX crafted.

### use item used how many times used
USE_ITEM - Requires material parameter (item material name, e.g. BOW). Times the specified item was used. how many times used XX.

### break item broken how many broken
BREAK_ITEM - Requires material parameter (item material name, e.g. DIAMOND_PICKAXE). Times the specified item was broken. how many XX broken.

## Block-Parameter Statistics (BLOCK Type)

### mine block mined how much ore mined
MINE_BLOCK - Requires material parameter (block material name, e.g. DIAMOND_ORE). Times the specified block was mined. how much XX ore mined, how many diamonds mined.

## Entity-Parameter Statistics (ENTITY Type)

### kill entity how many zombies killed creepers killed
KILL_ENTITY - Requires entity_type parameter (entity type name, e.g. ZOMBIE, CREEPER, ENDER_DRAGON). Times the specified entity was killed. how many zombies killed, how many creepers killed, how many ender dragons killed.

### killed by entity killed by zombies
ENTITY_KILLED_BY - Requires entity_type parameter (entity type name, e.g. ZOMBIE, SKELETON). Times killed by the specified entity. how many times killed by zombies, how many times killed by skeletons.
